# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Hiring managers, engineering leads, and technical recruiters evaluating Martin Romano as a senior software engineer. Context: viewed on desktop in a professional setting; occasionally on mobile. Job: get a rapid, credible impression of skills, experience, and personality.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio for Martin Romano, senior software engineer. Communicates technical depth, sharp UX sensibility, and shipping credibility. Success = interview conversation or direct outreach.

## Brand Personality
Technical, precise, sharp. The design is the demonstration: grid systems, monospace details, and intentional restraint that shows without explaining.

## Anti-references
Minimal all-white or cream portfolios with no color personality — tasteful-but-invisible, forgettable against a crowded field.

## Design Principles
1. Show, don't describe — technical craft in the implementation is the pitch.
2. Precision over decoration — every detail earns its place; nothing is ambient.
3. Commitment over safety — the orange, the grid, the uppercase type are POV, not options.
4. Accessible by default — WCAG 2.1 AA is the floor, not the ceiling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. Known motion sensitivity concern: reduced-motion preference must be respected for all CSS animations, not just GSAP. No specific colour-blindness constraints.
